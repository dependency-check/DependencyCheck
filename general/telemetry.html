Telemetry

Scarf

OWASP dependency-check uses Scarf to collect anonymous usage data to help us understand how the software is being used and how we can improve it. You can opt out of telemetry collection by setting the environment variable SCARF_NO_ANALYTICS or DO_NOT_TRACK to true.
